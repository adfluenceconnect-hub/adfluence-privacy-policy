Overview & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Adfluence Digital ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects personal information submitted by prospective property buyers and investors through Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Lead Ads, Instant Forms, WhatsApp inquiries, phone calls, and landing pages.

Adfluence Digital operates real estate marketing and buyer inquiry campaigns across Telangana and India, connecting interested homebuyers and plot investors with verified residential ventures, developers, and authorized project sales teams.

2

Information We Collect

When you submit a Meta Instant Form on Facebook or Instagram, click to message us on WhatsApp, or request a callback, we only collect information you voluntarily provide:

  • •
    Contact Details Full Name, Phone Number, WhatsApp Number, and Email Address (when provided).
  • •
    Real Estate Preferences Preferred property type, plot size (such as 240 sq. yd. residential plots), location corridor (e.g., ORR Exit 4 & Beeramguda), and purchase timeline.
  • •
    Site Visit Scheduling Preferred date, day of the week, or time slot to visit the featured project or layout.
  • •
    Communication Records Follow-up notes from phone calls or WhatsApp chats regarding brochures, layout maps, or municipal approvals.
3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through our Meta Lead Ads and Instant Forms strictly to respond to your real estate inquiry and connect you with the relevant project team:

  • Contacting you via phone call, SMS, email, or WhatsApp regarding the specific property or open-plot venture you inquired about.
  • Sharing project brochures, current plot availability, pricing details, layout maps, Google Maps location pins, and approval/registration information.
  • Coordinating and confirming guided site visits with authorized project advisors.
  • Managing lead quality, preventing duplicate follow-ups, and maintaining accurate inquiry records.
4

Data Sharing, Retention & Security

No Sale of Personal Data: Adfluence Digital never sells, rents, or trades your phone number, email, or inquiry details to unrelated third-party list brokers or spam marketers.

Authorized Project Partners & Tools: Because Adfluence Digital runs real estate lead-generation campaigns on behalf of specific property ventures, your inquiry details are shared strictly with the authorized real estate developer, venture owner, or project sales team for the exact property you expressed interest in, as well as secure CRM and communication platforms (such as Meta Business Suite and WhatsApp Business) used to service your request.

Data Retention & Safeguards: We retain your inquiry details only for as long as necessary to fulfill your property request, coordinate site visits, or comply with applicable legal obligations, after which records are securely deleted or anonymized. We use access-controlled systems and encrypted communication tools to protect your personal data.

5

Your Rights & Opt-Out Choices

In accordance with applicable Indian data protection laws—including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)—you remain in control of your personal data and have the right to:

  • Withdraw Consent & Opt Out Anytime: Ask us to stop calling or messaging you on WhatsApp by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp, emailing Adfluence.connect@gmail.com, or informing our advisor during a call.
  • Access & Correction: Request confirmation of the personal details we hold for you or ask us to correct inaccurate contact information.
  • Data Erasure / Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your lead details from our active records when you no longer wish to be contacted.
